AIMCHAOS

Aim. Shoot. Outsmart your friends.

How it works
  • 5 rounds, 30 to 45 seconds each. A different mini-game every round.
  • Shoot targets. Chain hits for combos. Combos multiply score.
  • Every 6 hits in a row earns a power-up. Press 1 2 3 to fire it at the leader, or click it to pick a victim.
  • Missing costs a little. Shooting a fake, a friend, or the wrong target costs a lot.
